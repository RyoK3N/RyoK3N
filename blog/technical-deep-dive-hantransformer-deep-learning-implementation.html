The key algorithmic approaches in HANTransformer involve the integration of hierarchical attention mechanisms with the Transformer model. In the document classification task, this means processing sentences within documents and then aggregating these sentence-level features to form document-level representations. The Transformer model, on the other hand, is used to capture long-range dependencies and contextual information across the entire document. The combination of these two approaches allows the model to effectively encode the hierarchical structure of the text data. Specifically, the HANTransformer architecture includes embedding layers, attention mechanisms at both sentence and document levels, and a final classification layer. The Transformer model's self-attention mechanism is applied at the sentence level, while hierarchical attention is used at the document level to aggregate sentence representations.
